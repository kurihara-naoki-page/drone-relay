Drone Relay – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Drone Relay is a browser game made by Naoki Kurihara, a solo developer based in Japan. This page explains what the game does and does not do with your data.

What the game itself collects

Nothing personal. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no forms, and no analytics or tracking code of its own. It does not send any data to a server.

What is stored on your device

The game keeps one item in your browser's local storage: your best score (the number of packages delivered, the week reached, and the time it was set). This stays on your device and is never transmitted. You can remove it at any time by clearing the site data for this page in your browser.

When you play on a game portal

If you play Drone Relay on a third-party game portal (for example, a site that embeds the game and shows advertisements), that portal may use its own software to show ads, measure play sessions, or remember your preferences. Such processing is governed by the portal's own privacy policy, not by this one. The game does not receive personal data from the portal.

Hosting

The standalone version of the game is served from GitHub Pages. As with any website, the hosting provider may record technical information such as your IP address in its server logs. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever starts to handle data differently (for example, online leaderboards), this page will be updated and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the game's GitHub repository.
